WHO IT'S FOR
1. Curious newcomers on a phone who have never placed a trade and bounce off seed phrases and order books. They get a passkey wallet in two taps, a free practice lap on the live market, then $5 positions with a stop on every ride.
2. Casual traders who already play short "up or down" markets. They get a faster, physical version with real order-book fills and receipts instead of bots on the other side.
3. Existing Perpl traders. They connect their account with a trade-only key and get a coach, rails and a pit wall on top of the venue they already use.
4. Venues and wallets (B2B). The worlds talk to venues through two small seams (a price/flow feed and an executor), so any Monad order book can plug in and get consumer flow that arrives by playing.

WHY THE DEMAND IS REAL
- Retail is moving to short, game-like formats: Polymarket's 5-minute crypto Up/Down markets reached about 67% of its crypto up/down volume within weeks of launch (Tekedia, Mar 2026).
- Plain spot is cooling while event-style products explode: Robinhood's July 2026 crypto volume was down 62% year over year while event contracts rose 20x (FX News Group, Aug 2026).
- Perps inside a consumer, mostly mobile wallet work: Phantom's perps reached $39.4B volume and 137,496 active wallets in under a year (Solana Compass, Jun 2026).
- Monad has the venue and the users: perps volume passed $2.5B in August 2026, a network record, across 4.2M+ wallets (Monad blog, Sep 2026).
- Passkeys remove the drop-off: passkey sign-ins succeed 93% of the time vs 63% for other methods, in 8.5s instead of 31.2s (FIDO Alliance Passkey Index).

ACQUISITION
- Free practice is the top of the funnel: a link in a chat lands straight in a ride, no wallet needed.
- Every ride is content: drives can be clipped and shared, and every receipt carries real Monad tx links. A share card per ride and creator / stream mode turn players into distribution (racing and surfing on a live chart is made for TikTok, Shorts and streams).
- Referral links give invited riders a points boost; the Paddock leaderboard and Championship reward real volume, verified onchain.
- Ecosystem distribution: co-marketing with Perpl, Kuru and Agora, Monad community quests, and an embeddable ride widget for wallets and apps ("in-app trading experiences inside games and other apps").
- A launch audience on day one: the founder's existing crypto education channels (web3wikis on X, AirdropSea guides).

RETENTION
- Short sessions with a clear end: a ride lasts 1 to 10 minutes and ends with a receipt, a habit loop rather than a terminal left open.
- The daily set and streaks (the streak mechanic that Duolingo credits with moving company-wide daily users) give a reason to come back every day.
- Progress you can see: the next-move ladder, volume clubs, the Logbook and the Championship.
- Learning that compounds: the pit wall and pit radio teach in context (storms, spreads, funding, fee drag), so riders get better instead of burning out.
- Trust as retention: rails on every ride (capped clips, stop, take-profit, max loss, flatten on exit) mean one bad day doesn't end someone's journey.
